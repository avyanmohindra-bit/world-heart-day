📊 Understanding Cardiovascular Metrics

Knowledge is your first line of defense against cardiovascular disease (CVD). Keep a close watch on these critical biological markers:

Blood Pressure (BP)

Measures the force of blood pushing against artery walls.

  • Normal: Under 120/80 mmHg
  • Elevated: 120-129 / under 80 mmHg
  • Hypertension: 130+/80+ mmHg

Resting Heart Rate

The number of times your heart beats per minute while at complete rest.

  • Typical Range: 60–100 beats per minute
  • Athletic Range: 40–60 beats per minute

Cholesterol Profiles

Fatty substances in the blood that affect arterial health.

  • HDL (Good): Helps clear pathways
  • LDL (Bad): Can cause plaque blockages

🚨 Critical Warning Signs & Response

Recognizing an Acute Cardiac Event

Cardiovascular incidents demand rapid response. Watch for these conditions immediately:

  • Chest Pain/Pressure: A feeling of tightness, heavy squeezing, or crushing pain in the center of the chest.
  • Radiating Pain: Discomfort spreading outward to the shoulders, neck, jaw, or down the left arm.
  • Respiratory Distress: Sudden shortness of breath, with or without chest discomfort.
  • Systemic Signs: Cold sweats, unexpected dizziness, or sudden lightheadedness.

Action Step: If these signs appear, do not wait. Call emergency services immediately. Quick intervention preserves heart muscle tissue.
